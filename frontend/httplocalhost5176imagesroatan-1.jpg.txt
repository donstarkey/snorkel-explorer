The Roatán Blue Channel is a popular dive and snorkel site located just off the shore of West End Village on the northwest side of Roatán, Honduras. It is a natural break in the reef crest, situated roughly 200 yards out, west of Tita’s Pink Seahorse and near the Luna Beach Resort.Key Details About Blue Channel:Location: West End, parallel to the shore, easily accessed by boat from West End Village or as a long swim from shore.Depth: The site is relatively shallow, with the channel floor around 30 feet, and a reef ridge sloping from 10 to 45 feet.Features: Famous for its swim-throughs, coral walls, and marine life like damselfish, moray eels, and school of silversides (often seen in Aug/Sept).Landmark: It is located directly west of Tita's Pink Seahorse and is frequently visited from nearby locations like Blue Bahia.
